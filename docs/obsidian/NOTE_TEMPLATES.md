# Obsidian Note Templates

These templates are safe to copy into the Obsidian vault. They avoid secrets and keep
business facts separate from assumptions.

## Project note

```markdown
# Project Name

## Owner

## Current status

## Goal

## Links
- GitHub:
- Dashboard:
- Related PRs:

## Decisions

## Blockers

## Next actions
```

## Product idea note

```markdown
# Product Idea

## Confirmed facts

## Assumptions to verify

## Target buyer

## ESA/funding considerations

## Required contents

## Price basis

## Launch blockers
```

## Decision note

```markdown
# Decision: Title

## Date

## Decision

## Why

## Alternatives considered

## Follow-up tasks
```
