# Dashboard Widgets

These dashboard widgets are the first useful views to build once OmniRoute can read GitHub,
Obsidian-selected docs, and storefront launch status.

## Preparation Station launch status

Shows whether the storefront is review-only, ESA-launch candidate, or public-ready.

## Open PRs

Shows branch name, PR title, status, check results, and whether owner approval is needed.

## Build status

Shows the most recent build command, result, commit, and generated-page status.

## ESA product readiness

Tracks SKU, title, price basis, age range, contents, shipping expectations, and funding
copy review.

## General Store preview status

Confirms General Store remains preview-only until real direct-commerce requirements are
complete.

## Launch blockers

Shows unresolved blockers from launch docs, audits, PR comments, and operator decisions.

## Next operator actions

Lists the highest-priority human actions: approve PR, confirm product facts, provide vendor
status, connect payment, verify policy, or choose deployment path.
