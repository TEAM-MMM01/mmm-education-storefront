How TEFA Funds Work

Every program works a little differently. Here's a general overview for Texas families.

1

Apply and Qualify

Submit your TEFA application through the Texas Comptroller's Odyssey portal at educationfreedom.texas.gov. Once approved, you'll receive access to the Odyssey marketplace.

2

Find Preparation Station

Check the Odyssey marketplace for currently available Preparation Station offerings. Vendor approval does not guarantee search placement, category placement, or offering availability.

3

Select a Credit Amount

Choose the credit amount that meets your learner's needs. Funding timelines are set by the program administrator.

4

Shop and Learn

Use TEFA funds only for offerings that are currently available and eligible through Odyssey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TEFA?

The Texas Education Freedom Account (TEFA) provides eligible families with state-funded education dollars that can be used for approved educational expenses, including curriculum, textbooks, tutoring, and online programs.

How do I purchase from Preparation Station?

All TEFA purchases happen through the Odyssey marketplace. Check Odyssey for currently available Preparation Station offerings and complete any eligible transaction there.

Is Preparation Station approved for TEFA?

Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC (operating as Preparation Station) is an approved TEFA Marketplace vendor. Offerings are reviewed before appearing in the Odyssey marketplace. We do not describe products as TEFA-eligible without current, product-specific evidence.

Can I use TEFA for any product on your site?

Not all products may qualify. TEFA funds have specific approved expense categories. Check the Odyssey marketplace for which Preparation Station offerings are currently eligible.

What about PDSES or ClassWallet?

Our PDSES/ClassWallet status is currently unknown. We do not advertise these as approved until evidence is confirmed.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email the contact page. Complete requests are acknowledged within one business day.
