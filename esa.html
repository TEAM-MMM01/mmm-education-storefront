Frequently Asked Questions

What is TEFA?

The Texas Education Freedom Account (TEFA) provides eligible families with state-funded education dollars that can be used for approved educational expenses, including curriculum, textbooks, tutoring, and online programs.

How do I purchase from Preparation Station?

All TEFA purchases happen through the Odyssey marketplace. Search for "Preparation Station" or "Nationwide Acquisitions" in Odyssey, select your credit amount, and complete the transaction there.

Is Preparation Station approved for TEFA?

Yes. Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC is an approved TEFA Marketplace vendor. Each offering is reviewed separately in Odyssey. We do not describe products as TEFA-eligible without current, product-specific evidence.

Can I use TEFA for any product on your site?

Not all products may qualify. TEFA funds have specific approved expense categories. Check the Odyssey marketplace for which Preparation Station offerings are currently eligible.

What about PDSES or ClassWallet?

Our PDSES/ClassWallet status is currently unknown. We do not advertise these as approved until evidence is confirmed.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email mmminvestment25@gmail.com. All requests are acknowledged within one business day.
