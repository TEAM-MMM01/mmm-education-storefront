AI Literacy Bench Kit

Starts offline. What a model actually is, where it fails, and how not to be fooled by one — before the student ever opens a chatbot unsupervised.

Price pending Odyssey review pending

Ages 10–18 Format Physical kit + guide Ships Confirmed before purchase
What's in the box
  • How models really work, explained without jargon
  • Prompt-practice deck: asking well, then checking the answer
  • Spot-the-fake drills for generated images, audio, and text
  • Digital-footprint audit worksheet
  • Capstone project card: build one small, genuinely useful tool

Adding to this list does not charge anything. TEFA purchases happen in Odyssey after the offering is approved; this site only records a product-information request until then.

Why this one

Handing a young person an open chatbot and hoping for the best isn't education, and it isn't safe. This kit is built to make the student the one asking the hard questions — accounts stay with the parent, sessions are logged and shareable, and nothing personal about the student ever goes into a public tool.

Roughly half the kit happens on paper, in conversation, or out checking whether a claim survives contact with reality, before a screen is ever involved. Confidence follows capability here, not the other way around.
