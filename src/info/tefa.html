For families

From request to Odyssey in four steps

No payment is collected on this site. TEFA purchases and official order history stay in Odyssey.

  1. Browse the catalog. Pathways show SKU, purpose, and listing price for offering review. Kits stay under review.
  2. Open the product page. Each card links to what you get, use cases, and what is not included.
  3. Submit a request. Tell us the learner's age and goals. We confirm the pathway in writing — no payment or program-account details here.
  4. Purchase in Odyssey only after that exact SKU is listed as available. Vendor approval is not item approval.

Reviewer summary

What we provide

Preparation Station develops digital-first curriculum pathways across practical life, trade exposure, self-direction, creative design, technology readiness, and homeschool support, together with selected books and activity-based titles.

Reviewer summary

Purchase lanes are separated

Family direct-retail, TEFA-funded, and school or district purchasing follow distinct operational paths. TEFA-funded purchases occur only through the official Odyssey Marketplace for individually approved offerings.

Vendor status & compliance

Approval applies to the vendor — not to every offering

Preparation Station is an approved TEFA Marketplace vendor through Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC. Approval of the vendor does not mean every catalog item is approved, published, or available for TEFA-funded purchase.

Individual offerings appear in the marketplace only after program review. Marketplace participation is not a government endorsement of any product, and inquiry forms should not be used to submit payment details or sensitive records.

Catalog status

Public catalog is review-safe

The public catalog may display titles, domains, and planning concepts before a specific offering is released for purchase. When a title is still pending review or launch readiness, the site keeps that status visible and does not publish a live buy path.

Pricing policy

No unverified prices are published

Preparation Station publishes owner-approved pathway listing prices for offering review (see the catalog pathways). Kits and reserved books do not carry a public price until that exact SKU is verified in Odyssey.

Information control

Repository-controlled source of truth

Reviewer-facing summary information is maintained here in repository-controlled pages so changes are reviewable, auditable, and aligned with the published storefront and release checks.

Offering readiness

How offering status should be interpreted

A title shown on the site may be in one of several states: planning concept, under internal preparation, under offering review, approved and marketplace listed, or direct-retail only.

Unless a specific offering is explicitly identified as approved and available through the official TEFA marketplace, families and reviewers should treat it as not yet available for TEFA-funded purchase.

Reviewer notes

What is intentionally not claimed here

  • No kit or book price presented as Odyssey checkout.
  • No statement that every catalog title is TEFA-approved.
  • No PDSES / ClassWallet approval claim until confirmed in writing.
  • No payment or program-account collection on this site.
