Reviewer summary

Purchase paths are separated

Family direct-retail, TEFA-funded, and school/district purchasing follow distinct routes. TEFA-funded purchases happen only through the official Odyssey Marketplace using individually approved offerings.

Vendor status & compliance

Approval applies to the vendor — not to every offering

Preparation Station is an approved TEFA Marketplace vendor. Individual offerings appear in the marketplace only after program review, and no curriculum is described as TEFA-approved until that exact offering has been reviewed and appears in the marketplace. Marketplace participation is not a government endorsement of any product. No sensitive records or payment details should be submitted through inquiry channels.

Purchase-lane distinction

Direct retail

Direct checkout is disabled until pricing, fulfillment, and payment are fully configured and tested. Standard-retail discounts do not apply to TEFA-funded transactions.

Purchase-lane distinction

TEFA marketplace

TEFA-funded purchases must happen through the official Odyssey Marketplace using an individually approved offering. Marketplace terms apply to those purchases.

Purchase-lane distinction

Schools & districts

Institutional purchases follow a quote path: curriculum review, flexible licensing conversation, purchasing readiness, and responsible implementation.

FAQ — review context

Questions reviewers may have

Is Preparation Station an approved TEFA vendor?

Yes. Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC (operating as Preparation Station) is an approved TEFA Marketplace vendor based on the operator-provided approval email.

Does vendor approval make every offering TEFA-eligible?

No. Each offering is reviewed separately in the Odyssey portal. A curriculum is described as TEFA-approved only after that exact offering has been reviewed and appears in the marketplace.

What does the published pricing include?

Pricing is published per curriculum pathway tier. Inclusions are documented with each offering during its Odyssey review; no inclusion claim is made before that.

How do district purchases differ from TEFA purchases?

TEFA purchases happen through the official Odyssey Marketplace with an individually approved offering. Schools and districts use a separate quote path with licensing conversation.

Why do some offerings still show "offering review"?

Because no offering is published until its exact marketplace record is confirmed. Titles, formats, and pricing are subject to change until then.

What are the support and service boundaries?

Complete requests are acknowledged within one business day. PDSES/ClassWallet status is currently unknown and is not advertised as approved until evidence is confirmed.
