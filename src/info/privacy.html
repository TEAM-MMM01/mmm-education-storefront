Privacy policy

We collect almost nothing. Here is exactly what happens.

Preparation Station is a static information site. There are no accounts, no checkout, and no payment processing on this site. The Order page shows a preview cart for planning only — nothing there is charged, ordered, or reserved.

What we collect

Only what you send us

  • Your name and email, when you contact us or send a product request.
  • Your message content — used only to answer your request.
  • Nothing else. No accounts, no payment data, no checkout information.
What we don't do

No tracking, no selling

  • We do not run analytics, ads, or third-party trackers on this site.
  • We never sell or share your information.
  • Your starter list in the Mission Guide stays in your own browser only.
TEFA & Odyssey

Program data stays in the program

Purchases made with TEFA funds happen in the official Odyssey Marketplace under Odyssey's own privacy terms. Preparation Station does not receive your Odyssey account credentials, order history, or student records.

If we ever begin collecting more — for example, through a verified request form or an order import — this policy will be updated and dated before that happens.
