About the mission

We help families build the skills, habits, and judgment that strengthen the next generation.

Preparation Station is operated by Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC. We publish practical curriculum pathways and kits so learners can practice independent living, money habits, career readiness, respectful communication, self-command, digital judgment, and making — without pretending this is therapy or a live checkout.

What learners build

Capability, not worksheets-only

Pathways are organized around practical life, career and entrepreneurship, money habits, independent living, communication, self-advocacy, organization, self-discipline, conflict navigation, de-escalation as life-skill learning, digital judgment, AI literacy, and design.

How purchase works

Exact SKU, then Odyssey

Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC is an approved TEFA Marketplace vendor. Individual offerings are reviewed separately. TEFA-funded purchases are available only for the exact offering published in Odyssey. This website does not process TEFA payments.

How we help

A real person, in writing

Use the Mission Guide for a shortlist, then request a written pathway recommendation. We reply within one business day with current status and the correct purchase path.
