About the mission

Preparation Station helps families steer skill-building the way they steer the rest of their lives — with a plan.

Preparation Station is operated by Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC, an approved TEFA Marketplace vendor. We build project kits, workbooks, and curriculum guides for practical skills, self-command, design, and emerging technology — and we help families route approved purchases through the official Odyssey Marketplace.

What we make

Hands-on learning

Eighteen catalog items across five departments: Practical & Trade, Situation Handling & Self-Command, Design & Motion Studio, AI & Emerging Tech Bench, and Homeschool Essentials.

How we sell

Through approved programs

TEFA purchases happen in Odyssey. Each offering is reviewed separately before any family can buy it with program funds. No payment is collected on this site.

How we help

Guided selection

The Mission Guide matches age band and goals to catalog items, builds a starter list, and hands off to funding help when a purchase pathway needs confirming.

Where things stand

  • TEFA Marketplace vendor approval — confirmed for Nationwide Acquisitions, LLC.
  • Catalog offerings — all 18 items are under offering review; none is for sale yet.
  • Prices — no price is published until a verified catalog record exists.
  • PDSES/ClassWallet — status not yet confirmed; we do not advertise it as approved.
