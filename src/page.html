How funded purchase works

Four steps, one confirmed path.

There is no live checkout here on purpose. We confirm the correct official pathway in writing before any purchase, and we never collect payment on this site.

  1. Step 01

    Review an approved offering

    Find the product you're interested in and confirm its current offering and eligibility status. We say plainly when an offering hasn't completed review.

  2. Step 02

    Submit a request

    Send your request with the SKU, program name, and general details. Do not email account numbers, disability records, school identifiers, or financial documents.

    Confirm the pathway

    We acknowledge a complete request within one business day and confirm the purchasing pathway, price, availability, and delivery timeline in writing.

  4. Step 04

    Purchase through Odyssey

    TEFA purchases happen in Odyssey. Fulfillment begins on the written timeline after the order is accepted there.

Questions families ask
Do you only work with Texas families?

Preparation Station currently centers its program information on Texas. Shipping coverage for an ordinary self-pay request is confirmed on the written quote. We do not claim another state's program approval without current documentation.

Are you a school, a tutor, or a store?

A store. We supply tools, kits, and equipment along with the project books that make them worth owning. We don't enrol students, run classes, or issue grades — which also means your funds go toward materials your student keeps rather than hours that end.

I don't have an education savings account yet. Can you help?

We can identify the applicable official program resources and explain our product-request process. We do not decide eligibility, approve an account, guarantee an award, or ask you to email sensitive application records.

How do I know an item is eligible before I buy it?

For TEFA, purchase only offerings shown as available in the official Odyssey Marketplace. Each offering is reviewed separately; a vendor approval alone does not approve every item displayed on a vendor website.

Why is there no checkout button?

TEFA purchases happen in Odyssey. Preparation Station's separate direct checkout remains disabled until product prices, tax, shipping, returns, payment, and fulfillment are configured. This site never collects payment or program account information.

Can I buy without using program funds?

Direct purchase is planned but not active. No card information is collected on this site. A direct purchase path will open only for verified retail products, separate from TEFA and PDSES/ClassWallet.

What ages are these for?

Broadly 8–18, graded by readiness rather than grade level. The tool roll and the electronics bench assume supervision at the younger end; the design, motion, and skill-to-income kits land best from about 13 up. Tell us the student and we'll steer you.

What's your returns policy?

The final product-specific return and cancellation policy will be shown in Odyssey or the direct checkout before purchase. No general return promise is active yet.

Do you make an environmental claim on purchases?

No active environmental percentage, offset, donation, or shipping claim is included in a sale. Any future program will require written terms and supporting records.
